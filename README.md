Description
Located in Nazimabad, Karachi, this co-ed campus delivers structured instruction from Play Group through Grade 8 under the Karachi Board. Parents seeking reliable primary and middle-tier options receive an organized educational setting for young boys and girls. The learning setup focuses on building solid foundational understanding across all basic subjects.

Instructors deliver simple daily lessons that make routine textbook topics easy to comprehend. Students gain access to important facilities including a computer lab, library, creative art section, and music room. The school also integrates modern STEM education alongside traditional Islamic education classes, helping children develop technical capabilities alongside personal ethics.

Monthly fee starts from PKR 2000. Contact the school for admission details.
